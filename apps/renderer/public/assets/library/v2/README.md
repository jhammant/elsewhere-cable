# Asset Library V2 Image Batch

These assets were created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 31 July
2026 for Elsewhere Cable. Every scene and object is a wholly fictional, original project
asset. Prompts explicitly excluded named programmes, artists, performers, brands,
characters, network identities, readable text and watermarks.

Background plates use a wide 16:9 composition, empty actor positions and broadcast-safe
negative space. Prop sources were rendered against flat green chroma and converted to
alpha PNGs with the ImageGen skill's soft-matte and despill helper. No real people or
scraped media are used. The shadow-union office includes invented paper silhouettes only.

## Paper bus-stop kitchen

> A wholly original flat paper-cut suburban kitchen from an impossible reality, where
> every cupboard is slightly too narrow and a tiny indoor bus stop occupies the breakfast
> nook; handmade layered construction paper and felt, visible cut edges, open foreground,
> separate actor positions, cheerful morning light and faint bureaucratic unease.

## Indoor-rain weather studio

> A wholly original miniature practical weather studio during gentle indoor rain; tiny
> forecast desk, cardboard clouds, gutters routed through filing cabinets and one dry
> circle marked only by lighting; tactile stop-motion materials, clear staging zones.

## Pixel railway courtroom

> A fictional railway small-claims courtroom inside a station waiting room; judge's bench
> made from stacked luggage and witness box made from a ticket machine; crisp original
> low-resolution pixel art, side-on stage and fluorescent bureaucracy.

## Shadow cavern newsroom

> A wholly original cavern newsroom where geological strata serve as news screens,
> stalactites form a desk canopy and a small underground sun hangs from a cable; layered
> shadow-puppet theatre, coloured gels, linen texture and strong readable silhouettes.

## Watercolour aquarium council

> An empty underwater municipal council chamber run for fish; curved civic desks,
> bubble-powered microphones, seaweed filing cabinets and a public gallery of vacant
> shells; hand-painted watercolour and ink with calm, ordinary, faintly melancholy staging.

## Fluorescent dental gameshow

> A ludicrous fluorescent dental game-show studio where contestants would guess which
> tooth owns which secret; rotating molar podiums, a velvet gum-shaped curtain and abstract
> prize objects; glossy practical foam scenery with an uncluttered lower-third area.

## Xerox shadow-union office

> An office for a union representing household shadows; battered desks, duplicated forms,
> wall pegs for absent silhouettes and an old photocopier; high-contrast black-and-white
> torn-paper collage, halftone grime, staples and correction-fluid marks.

## Stained-glass receipt shop

> A late-night shopping studio inside a tiny fictional cathedral devoted to lost household
> receipts; abstract appliance windows, product plinths and a velvet demonstration table;
> luminous original stained glass, symmetrical staging and no real religious iconography.

## Thermal night bakery

> A fictional overnight bakery monitored by a municipal heat-inspection channel; empty
> ovens, cooling racks, pastry conveyor and one suspiciously cold cabinet; false-colour
> thermographic image with readable hot and cold zones and no interface text.

## Isometric rotary call centre

> A fictional call centre where every desk connects to one enormous rotary telephone below

> isometric technical-manual illustration with no labels.

## Clay roundabout newsdesk

> miniature, handmade road markings and overcast local-bulletin light.
