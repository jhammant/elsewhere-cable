# Photographic cutout pilot

These three raster assets were generated for Elsewhere Cable with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool on 30 July 2026. The people are fictional and do not depict public figures or
recognisable performers.

The pilot deliberately uses a single original programme. The renderer loads the background plate
and two transparent full-body character plates ahead of playback, then applies inexpensive
limited animation: independent head hinges, snap poses, camera motion and three-state mouth
movement. If an asset does not load, the existing procedural collage renderer remains available.

Prompts requested:

- An empty, photorealistic low-budget municipal public-access studio inside an impossible overnight
  laundromat, with no logos or readable text.
- A fictional municipal presenter in a burgundy second-hand suit, photographed full-body on a
  flat green background.
- A distinct fictional laundromat caller in a teal utility cardigan, photographed full-body on a
  flat green background.

The two character plates were converted locally from chroma-key sources to alpha PNGs.
